# Chronicle of Asterra

## Turn 0 — The Threshold at Glassroot

The Wayfarer arrived through the Morrowgate, a black-crystal arch tangled in the roots of Glassroot. The gate closed without explanation. Three routes opened into the unknown: the Whispering Orchard, Emberfall, and the Underdeep.

A Moth-Crowned Ferryman waits beside a dry basin. One silver moth carries a tiny blue flame. The Ferryman has not yet named their price.

**Roll:** none — the opening scene is a choice, not a contest.  
**Consequence:** pending the Wayfarer's first decision.
