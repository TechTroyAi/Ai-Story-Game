# Chronicle of Asterra

## Turn 0 — The Threshold at Glassroot

The Wayfarer arrived through the Morrowgate, a black-crystal arch tangled in the roots of Glassroot. The gate closed without explanation. Three routes opened into the unknown: the Whispering Orchard, Emberfall, and the Underdeep.

A Moth-Crowned Ferryman waited beside a dry basin. One silver moth carried a tiny blue flame. The Ferryman had not yet named their price.

**Roll:** none — the opening scene was a choice, not a contest.

## Turn 1 — The Waiting Answer

**Player action:** “Stand still and wait.”  
**Roll:** raw 99 + 0 = **99**, mythic success.  

Stillness became an untrained form of threadcraft. Time held its breath around Glassroot. The dry basin filled with dark water, revealing a fourth path of white roots beneath the tree. The Ferryman lost one silver moth from their crown and granted the Wayfarer one truthful question before the first road is chosen.

The Equal Echo answered immediately: something beyond the First Scar noticed the pause. A black thread is learning the Wayfarer's shadow.

**Image:** `assets/turn-001-the-waiting-answer.png`

## Turn 2 — The Ferryman's Name

**Player action:** “Ask him, who is he.”  
**Roll:** raw 15 + 0 = **15**, failure with consequence.

The Ferryman revealed the name **Veyr** and his history as the keeper of the Starless River who ferried the old gods across the First Scar. His true name was taken as payment, leaving him as the debt those gods abandoned.

The truthful answer was compelled, but the low roll made it costly: the Wayfarer's iron coin gained a black-edged ring, and the shadow-thread marked the Wayfarer as Veyr's passenger. The mark can open one threshold and can also give Veyr's hunters a trail.
